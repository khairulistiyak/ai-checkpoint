Are your AI coding agents moving fast but breaking things? 🚧

We all love the speed of AI assistants, but let's be honest: giving an AI free rein over your entire codebase usually ends in a debugging nightmare. Hallucinations, overwritten logic, and scope creep are real problems.

That’s why we built **AI Checkpoint** — an open-source, local task ledger that forces AI to work with engineering discipline.

🔹 **Atomic Execution:** 1 Step = 1 File edited. No more chaotic changes.
🔹 **Auto-Verification:** Steps are checked before the AI moves forward.
🔹 **Real-Time Visibility:** A beautiful local dashboard lets you monitor progress, block rogue steps, and stay in control.

It’s time to stop babysitting your AI and start building with structure.
