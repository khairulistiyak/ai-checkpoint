# AI-Checkpoint - Character-Based 10-Second Stories

Here are 4 highly engaging, character-driven 10-second video scripts. These use relatable personas to showcase the power of **AI-Checkpoint**. 

---

## Story 1: The Stressed Dev (Focus: Time Machine)
**Character:** Alex (A tired, stressed developer surrounded by empty coffee cups)
**Target Length:** 10 Seconds
* **Visual:** Alex is pulling their hair out, staring at a screen full of red errors. Text overlay: "AI broke the codebase... again."
* **Audio (Voiceover):** "Let your AI code for hours, just to watch it hallucinate?"
* **Visual:** Alex types `./l cp back` in the terminal. The code magically rewinds and fixes itself. Alex smiles, relaxed. Text overlay: "Instant Rollback ⏪"
* **Audio (Voiceover):** "Not anymore. Use AI-Checkpoint's Time Machine to undo any mistake instantly."
