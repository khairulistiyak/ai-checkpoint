## Dev.to / Hashnode Blog Post

**Title:** I Built an Open-Source Ledger to Stop AI Agents from Destroying My Codebase

**Post Body:**

If you’ve been using AI coding assistants recently, you know the drill:
1. You ask the AI to build a feature.
2. It writes 500 lines of code across 10 different files.
3. Your app crashes.
4. You spend the next 2 hours debugging the AI's hallucinations.

I got tired of this cycle. AI is incredibly powerful, but it lacks discipline. It needs a manager. 

That’s why I built **AI Checkpoint**.

### What is AI Checkpoint?
AI Checkpoint is a local, open-source task ledger. It acts as a strict supervisor for your AI coding agents. Instead of letting the AI run wild, it forces the AI to work in **atomic, step-by-step chunks**.

### How it Works:
* **1 Step = 1 File:** The AI is only allowed to modify one file at a time.
* **Auto-Verification:** Before the AI can move to step 2, it must verify that step 1 was completed correctly without errors.
* **Local Dashboard:** It spins up a beautiful React dashboard on your `localhost` where you can watch the AI work in real-time, see the logs, and even block steps if it starts hallucinating.

### The Tech Stack
Built entirely with Node.js and React, it integrates seamlessly into your existing workflow via a simple CLI. No cloud lock-in. Everything stays on your machine.

### Try it out
It’s 100% open-source on GitHub. If you’re building with AI, I’d love for you to try it out and let me know what you think!
